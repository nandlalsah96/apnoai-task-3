About Us

Why people keep coming back.

Cafe Aroma started as a tiny kiosk with one coffee machine and one simple rule: never serve anything we would not eat ourselves.


Since 2018, we have grown into a friendly neighbourhood cafe where people come for fresh coffee, warm food and good conversations.


Whether you are meeting friends, working remotely or simply enjoying a quiet morning, Cafe Aroma is your place to slow down and enjoy something fresh.
